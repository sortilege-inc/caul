Where they stand (SET, S27)

  • In the World Above, at the lip of the moon-rupture, looking down through it on their own world. Talis burns below; the fire should not cross the water.
  • Splendor is the air here — ambient, no Umbra, late-afternoon light with no sun. Space and gravity bend: members hang at angles to each other, and distance grows as you chase it.
  • The break came mid-fight (resume in combat): a house-sized leaping predator (a giant "bush-baby") struck to severe and marked for death but still up; a much larger feline watching from deeper in, not yet engaged; leech-things dropping from the high branches. The realm's least creatures are deadly.
  • Sylvie can teleport to anywhere she can picture clearly and accurately — a world-eye reach, from having seen the whole world from the rupture.
  • Heyou's heart yields Splendor essence slowly; a vial is in Draz's keeping.
  • Unstuck from reality: while it holds, every Hope spent and every armor/HP/stress marked counts double; a character clears it with an action (roll varies) or a met condition. OPEN: whether it persists once they leave the World Above.

Beat 1 — Inola, to be rescued

  • SET (S24): Inola blessed Draz ("my son"), handed him the Sacred Pyre scepter, would have caged the Runt God as a Splendor source, then stole a book of planar passage from the Forbidden Library and stepped through a Splendor door. Unseen since.
  • MINE: a Splendor door, opened by one hunting the source of Splendor, let out into the Splendor realm. She is here, and cannot get out on her own — found as the session's first pull.
  • MINE: freeing her returns two things to the company: her book of planar passage (aiming a way between worlds on purpose, to pair with Sylvie's world-eye), and the live "my son" claim on Draz.
  • OPEN: what holds her — a god's site, a predator's lair, or the broken geometry itself. What she wants now, and whether she is an ally or still means to cage a source.

Beat 2 — Steal from a god

  • SET (S5): the departed gods — Tlellic, Kreth, Brathis, Mollthain, Caranthalis, Ossa, Ulmira, God-King Otheidas.
  • SET (S10): Ygva wants remnants and totems of the eight, to eight altars, to draw the gods down and bind them; the piles for Kreth, Brathis and Mollthain were thin.
  • SET (S27): the gods ascended here.
  • Opportunity: take a god's remnant, totem, or essence from the World Above.
  • MINE: the god is Ossa, the Wanderer of the Peaks — the realm is curving, floating peaks, and Draz's ever-upward compass already points to Ossa. What is taken is a relic of the Wanderer.
  • NOTE (S26): a god reached from the world below answers only as residue — a recording, not the living god. A relic here is more likely kept by megafauna, wards, or a residue than defended by the god in person.
  • OPEN: which god; what exactly is taken; whether it feeds Ygva's pyre or is a thing to withhold from her.

Beat 3 — Sylvie fixes the next destination

  • The move: books + sight from the World Above → one destination fixed accurately enough to open a door out. Play picks which.

The Amber Reach, living

  • SOURCE (S7): "far off west the ancient Amber Reach." SOURCE (S25): a painting showed "a city climbing a hill beneath giant archways"; the true-face paintings open onto a place as it was.
  • MINE: the door opens onto the Amber Reach when Splendor still ran — a dense, shining hill-city under great arches, its people of the lost golden age, Splendor grounded and ordered rather than wild. It is the seat from which God-King Otheidas issued the last writ (the pyre-order and the apology for leaving). What waits: a standing Sacred Pyre, and the chance to learn the pyres and the Ascension from the age that knew them — and, if Beat 2 is spent here instead, an Otheidas remnant.
  • OPEN: the past itself, or a pocket that never fell. Whether the company reads to its people as guests or as monsters (as the Talis fae saw them).
