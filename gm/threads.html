Immediate / Active

At the Celsian Athenaeum — where the party stands now

  • The party just fought off an Umbra incursion at the Astronomer's Spire (S24): with the Sacred Pyre thinned and the Keeper's light gone, an Oak Treant, Demonic Hound Packs, Stag Knights, and two Vampires broke in. They killed everything — but the compound is actively failing. The pyre no longer holds the dark; staff and students have fallen back to a defensible third of the grounds.
  • Inola is gone. She completed a ritual at the Spire and stepped through a Splendor planar door, taking the light with her. Sylvie found the gap on the shelves afterward: Inola had stolen a book of planar passage (gates + passwall) from the Forbidden Library and used it to leave. (GM: where to — the World Above, or elsewhere? And what is she really after?)
  • Vanco and Zevin (Draz's father and brother) are still below, finishing their own heist, promised to return. (GM: what is their heist? when do they resurface?)
  • The teleport is unlocked. Sylvie carried out paintings and academic works of the Amber Reach — the "true faces" she needed to blink somewhere she's never stood. (Destination decision — see "The road onward" below; GM leaning Thistleglass.)

The recovered books (identified, S24)

  • The king's book (Heyou lifted it from a Spectral Librarian): a ghost-written military treatise of an ancient warrior-king — his exploits and battlefield tactics. (Mechanical payoff TBD.)
  • Inola's book: a courtly text on a subtle social/influence magic; researching it could grant Sylvie a Grace-domain card (a domain no PC has).
  • The Nailed Tome = Ygva's depletion thesis. Her rejected draft / the evidence for her expulsion, detailing how to deplete objects and "containers" as ritual fuel — gruesome. Heyou copied it to magical parchment (the original crumbled to dust). This grants Sylvie Splendor rituals fuelled by depletion dice (d20 → d12 → … → d4, then spent — see the item/rules pass).

Speaker

It woke and followed her up, naming itself Speaker. (GM: what is it, what does it want, and is it kin to Kelun and the old talking constructs?)

World Threads

The Elect and the World Above

  • The Athenaeum's doctrine: only the Elect may ascend through a door to the World Above; the rest are left to entropy and the dark. Ygva named two candidates — Sylvie and Heyou. Otheidas tried to ascend his whole people and failed. (Central cosmology — where does this lead?)

The road onward — where to next

  • The teleport is now live: Sylvie has the Amber Reach "true faces," and under the Runt God's shield the party can reach places the archivists never could. Candidate destinations:
    • Thistleglass — the last unspent lead Ygva named (alongside Grithmaar Deep and the Athenaeum): "Faerie's Guard," tied to Kreth (False Visions / the Moon). (GM leaning here for the next session. Logistics: does Sylvie have a "true face" of Thistleglass, or do they reach it another way?)
    • The Amber Reach (west) — Draz's lineage, Heyou's-origins lead; true faces already in hand.
    • After Inola / the World Above — follow the Keeper through wherever her planar door led.
    • The Eye (below) — whatever the Spire was built to watch.
  • The expedition log Inola gave Heyou (60 of 80 lost) warns what waits in the far places.

The Eye in the hole in the sky

(GM: what is it — a god, a titan, a thing of the World Above, the same order as the Library's Floating Eye? What does being seen by it mean?)
