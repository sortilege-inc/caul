Immediate / Active

At the Celsian Athenaeum — where the party stands now

  • The party is resting inside the Athenaeum with Dagga Brunnvar and Pinchie, reading the two books recovered from the vaults (the ghost-book and the Nailed Tome — nature still to be decided).
  • The compound is no longer safe: Dean Therion Calanthe is dead (drained by Sylvie), the Runt God tore up through the Audience-Hall floor, and the settlement's Sacred Pyre was pulled and only hastily relit — a full relight is a week's work for Dagga and twenty hands.
  • Inola has vanished. She used the Forbidden-Library chaos to take something and slip away. (GM: what did she take, and where has she gone? Was she always after it?)
  • Vanco and Zevin (Draz's father and brother) have gone back down into the deep to finish their own unfinished heist, promising to return. (GM: what is their heist? when/how do they resurface?)
  • The next move is Sylvie's teleport. She can reach the far, guarded regions Inola showed on the world-map — but only if she is first shown their "true faces" (paintings / depictions). The library raid was to find these. This is the campaign's next horizon (west, toward the Amber Reach and beyond).

The recovered books (undetermined)

  • The ghost-book (Heyou lifted it from a Spectral Librarian and put it in Sylvie's hands) and the Nailed Tome (Sylvie took it from the dark below the eye, nailed shut). (GM tabled — to define.)

Speaker

  • A faceless stone thing in the dark below the Forbidden Library. Sylvie tried to wake it with Splendor (it refused) and then with a piece of her own Umbra (it took). It woke and followed her up, naming itself Speaker. (GM: what is it, what does it want, and is it kin to Kelun and the old talking constructs?)

Character Arcs

Sylvie — demigod, Umbra-witch, unburdened of the Godshard

  • RESOLVED (S15): Demigod transformation — absorbed Mollthain's Splendor essence via Damnation. Permanent: Ichor of the Gods (advantage die always d10); Weight of Divinity (fail-with-Fear → mark a Stress or give the GM a Fear).
  • RESOLVED (S17): gained teleportation — how the party travels now.
  • RESOLVED (S21 → S23): became the vessel of the Godshard (a god-fragment, drawn from the drained dean), then expelled it in the Forbidden Library, where the Floating Eye destroyed it. She "kept a shadow of her own in its place." (GM: what is that shadow — a lasting mark of having carried a god?)
  • Her heart is still missing. Ygva held it — and Ygva is now dead. Where is the heart? (OPEN — Ygva's death orphaned this thread.)
  • Stoneweaver origins / "Sister Moon": at the Stoneheart Peaks she took up a focus-stone that answered her, and learned her stone-magic always ran on instinct, not book-knowledge. The torn-out crown of her mountain is "part of the moon" now; her rites "called back the moon" (S17). Connection possibly tied to Kreth (False Visions / the Moon).

Draz — Otheidas blood, Beast Form, a family found

  • RESOLVED (S20-21): took Casper tol-Otheidas's heir-circlet → Splendor-sight (sees Splendor as light/aura); gained Beast Form (bird); relit the Athenaeum pyre with the scepter.
  • Otheidas blood: Inola named the circlet the crown of God-King Otheidas's heir and marvelled that both crown and Beast accepted him — "it is in your bloodline too." (Major: what does his descent from the god-king mean?)
  • Gentles the Runt God (teaching it to greet him softly).
  • Family (S22-23): found his father Vanco and brother Zevin frozen in the vaults. The truth: Vanco carried Zevin (one of two older twins) west to the Athenaeum; Draz's mother turned back for Draz with the second twin, and neither the mother nor that twin was ever seen again. (OPEN: the fate of the mother and second twin.)
  • Binding rune on his arm (Borek revealed it) — still uninvestigated. Compass steadied at "eleven o'clock," pointing toward Ossa. Heretic mark (inner bicep, see below).

Heyou — a construct, and the ones who made him

  • RESOLVED (S23): Vanco returned Heyou's original motive-core (drawn from his own chest). Vanco and Zevin had been sent to the Athenaeum specifically to learn how Heyou, a construct, was made. (Major lead into Heyou's origins.)
  • Carries a Suppression Amulet (from Dean Therion) — suppresses magic both ways; he is slowly learning to cast through it.
  • Chest sigil and an Otheidas-linked circlet matching it among the artifacts — unresolved. May be key to creating new beings; tied to Kelun and the stone constructs. Absorbed a portion of the Askavir Heart (holy power). Recovered leg marked with the symbol of Brathis — unexplored. Old Syndicate ties (Borek holds part of his essence).

Jamal — the last of Grithmaar Deep

  • RESOLVED (S16): came home to the drowned dwarven city, fought his risen kin, and watched Sylvie drink them. Emotional climax of his arc.
  • The Horned Iron Figure's telepathic threat ("you are among those meant to be destroyed… finish the job") — who targeted his people, and is it over? (OPEN.)
  • The chain of little lights that took to him from the slain Skeletal Dragon (S23) — (GM: what is this?)
  • Heretic mark (right buttock, see below). The party's anchor and most grounded member.

The Heretic Marks (slow burn — now 20+ sessions)

  • Both Draz (inner bicep) and Jamal (right buttock) bear concealed marks. What faith/order marks heretics, who hunts them, what was the heresy? Still completely unopened. (Consider seeding.)

Faction & NPC Threads

The Runt God — travelling with the party

  • The Heart of Stone = the Runt God: the runt of the gods, too small to ascend, that Sylvie once thrust into the sky. Draz gentles it; its reaching can kill; it devoured Casper tol-Otheidas. (What does it want? What happens as it grows and bonds with Draz?)

Ygva — dead

  • RESOLVED (S19): Ash-begets-Tide's chant turned her remains into two sapling treants; Inola's later resurrection failed. Ygva is gone. Her god-binding plan is leaderless — and her death orphaned Sylvie's missing heart, her cultivated Sacred Tree seeds, and the World Below pyre the titans were building for her.

Kaelyn and Rhys — the Stoneweavers she wronged

  • Sylvie's former fellow Stoneweavers, blaming her for a Midsummer rite gone violent and for sending them "up into the higher realm with the Runt God." Rhys is undead; wants to wrangle the Runt God to re-ascend. Kaelyn tends it. Sylvie cursed Rhys off ("go and die") at S19. (OPEN — they will be back.)

Inola Wending — the Keeper who walked off with something

  • Keeper of Splendor; guided the party through the vaults, then used the Library chaos to take something and vanish. (GM: what, why, and where to?)

Vanco & Zevin — gone deeper

  • Draz's father and brother; back down finishing their own heist. Vanco wears a token of Heyou's old Syndicate. (GM: their heist, and their return.)

Kelun and Divine Judgment (dormant, owed)

  • Kelun promised consequences for the destruction of the Sacred Tree (sacred to Caranthalis), and views the party as "useful components." Unseen since the Grithmaar arc. (When does the bill come due?)

Borek and the Syndicate (dormant)

  • Robbed and wounded; holds part of Heyou's essence; the Askavir-egg bargain never closed. The party has teleported far out of his reach — unresolved, not closed. The eye-patched katari among the Verrow's Span refugees may be his.

World Threads

The Elect and the World Above

  • The Athenaeum's doctrine: only the Elect may ascend through a door to the World Above; the rest are left to entropy and the dark. Ygva named two candidates — Sylvie and Heyou. Otheidas tried to ascend his whole people and failed. (Central cosmology — where does this lead?)

The far, guarded places (the road west)

  • Under the Runt God's shield, and with Sylvie's teleport, the party can reach distant regions the archivists never could — once Sylvie sees their "true faces." The Amber Reach (west; Draz's lineage) is the nearest. The expedition log Inola gave Heyou (60 of 80 lost) warns what waits.

The failing pyres & Drosven's Gate (dormant original quest)

  • The original mission — rekindle Drosven's Gate's pyre — was never completed; the party teleported away and never returned. Drosven's Gate is overrun, its people gone; Dolm's Break's pyre also failed (30 refugees at Verrow's Span). Whether the pyre-failures are coordinated is still unanswered. (Dormant, but the debt is unpaid.)

The World Below (dormant)

  • Titans building a colossal pyre under Ygva's (now dead) direction; the binding plan's fate uncertain. A dead island-city glimpsed through the luminous hole. Sylvie's lunar power is stronger there.

Sarru & Savel (loose — likely lost)

  • Captured, Umbra-mutated, and being transported back when the party still travelled overland. The party has since teleported across the world twice; their fate is unaddressed. (GM: abandoned at Grithmaar? Resolve or reintroduce.)

Dormant / Background

  • Bob and You Bastard — lost. You Bastard (the mount) drowned and Bob the Living Fortress sank, too great to move, in flooded Grithmaar Deep (S17). Heyou salvaged only the Blessed Branch and the Guardian's collar.
  • The Blessed Branch — still carried. The party's last branch; leverage for the abandoned pyre quest and a thing of value in itself.
  • Mellon and Yuki — spectral. Dead ranger and cat, now permanent spectral forms. Role unclear.
  • Pinchie — companion. Jamal's crab, collared at the Stoneheart Peaks; the party's mount since You Bastard drowned.
  • Refugees at Verrow's Span; Lila & Bufa. Thirty Dolm's Break refugees; Draz's great-aunt Lila and her west-ranging grandchild Bufa (intended future guides).
