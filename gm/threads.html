They won the fae's hospitality by meeting the Moonshadow's Mirror in a shatter-me contest rather than a slaughter. (GM: this is the repurposed Thistleglass encounter; where does the wood lead — a Blessed-Branch source, a Kreth lead, Draz's home coast?)
  • Left behind at the Athenaeum: the Runt God stays by the Sacred Pyre, in an uneasy truce brokered by Draz and Ash-begets-Tide with the priest's fungus, both to leash it and to try to feed the failing fire. Sylvie planted a Portal Seed in the Forbidden Library — a possible return-door / first node of a portal network.
  • Came along: Pinchie and Speaker (now changed — see below). Draz was carried and then slept the wood out (ate a fae fruit; see his arc).

  • Vanco and Zevin (Draz's father and brother) are still below the Athenaeum, finishing their own heist, promised to return. (GM: their heist? when do they resurface?)

The Faerie Wood & the Eyes of Kreth — S25

  • The wood's fae keep no Sacred Pyre yet are not Umbral monsters and not umbra-possessed — they chose the glamour. Their court answers to Moonshadow (a soft, child-sized thing) and the Shadow Cast (the great looming shadow that stands over it, with its own eyes). Both wear the Eyes of Kreth — pale moon-orbs set into gouged sockets, divine remnants of Kreth (deception & the moon), kin to the Blessed Branches and the Askavir as god-leavings.
  • The glamour is real for those inside it. Enveloped by the Shadow Cast, Heyou briefly saw through its eyes: the wood sunlit and teeming with life, his own companions rendered as ugly monsters fouling it, and the Shadow Cast itself no horror but a gentle beast. That is how the eye-bearers see the world — the radiant past, with the intruders as the monstrous ones. (The party now has a first-hand taste of what wearing Kreth's eyes does.)
  • Kreth "went away and left his eyes." Moonshadow says the god "had to fetch something — surely very big," and doesn't know if he'll return. (GM: what did Kreth leave to fetch? ties to the moon-as-membrane lore below.)
  • The Mirror is beaten by wit, not force: it set a "shatter me" contest (non-lethal), and Heyou won it by reshaping the mirror-glass in two rather than out-damaging it. The wood then extended the company its hospitality. (A door into the fae, not a war — the party brokered coexistence.)
  • Speaker is changed (major): during the fight Moonshadow set two Eyes of Kreth into Speaker's face, and afterward the mute construct rose and spoke in a full, sonorous voice it had never had: "Stop seeing. The truth is not always what is seen plainly. Use wisdom to decide whose eyes to trust." Then it went inert again — still wearing the eyes. (GM: is this Kreth speaking through it? Speaker now carries a god's organs of perception — what does that make it, and whose side is it on?)

The recovered books (identified, S24)

  • The king's book (Heyou lifted it from a Spectral Librarian): a ghost-written military treatise of an ancient warrior-king — his exploits and battlefield tactics. (Mechanical payoff TBD.)
  • Inola's book: a courtly text on a subtle social/influence magic; researching it could grant Sylvie a Grace-domain card (a domain no PC has).
  • The Nailed Tome = Ygva's depletion thesis. Her rejected draft / the evidence for her expulsion, detailing how to deplete objects and "containers" as ritual fuel — gruesome. Heyou copied it to magical parchment (the original crumbled to dust). This grants Sylvie Splendor rituals fuelled by depletion dice (d20 → d12 → … → d4, then spent — see the item/rules pass).

Speaker

It woke and followed her up, naming itself Speaker. (GM: what is it, what does it want, and is it kin to Kelun and the old talking constructs?)
  • S25 — it now wears the Eyes of Kreth and has spoken (see "The Faerie Wood & the Eyes of Kreth" above). An Umbra-woken construct now fitted with a moon-god's eyes, delivering warnings in a borrowed voice.

She is now openly entertaining apotheosis. (Central arc question — where does she take this?)
  • NEW (S25) — lore she now holds: the God-King's last writ is an apology that established the Sacred Pyres and admitted he'd be away and likely not return (no reason given). And the moon in pre-absence texts is not an object but a membrane over the whole sky that thins to bring visions — that is Kreth; the floating orbs are the Eyes of Kreth. She is chasing why the gods left and how to bring them back un-angered.

The Elect and the World Above

  • The Athenaeum's doctrine: only the Elect may ascend through a door to the World Above; the rest are left to entropy and the dark. Ygva named two candidates — Sylvie and Heyou. Otheidas tried to ascend his whole people and failed. (Central cosmology — where does this lead?)
