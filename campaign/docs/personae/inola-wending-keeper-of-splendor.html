Dramatis Persona

Inola Wending, Keeper of Splendor

Inola is a middle-aged Simiah known for her radiant white-hot Splendor and her role as a source of magical energy. She has a warm demeanor, welcoming others with open arms and referring to Sylvie as "dazzling." Inola occasionally retreats to the Barn for solitude, seeking respite from the busyness of the dean and students.

During a dinner with Sylvie, Inola discusses her role in the compound and the concept of Ascension, identifying Sylvie and Heyou as potential candidates. She introduces Casper tol-Otheidas, the great-grandson of the God-King Otheidas, and explains that only The Elect may ascend, emphasizing the necessity of self-salvation for those chosen. Inola's interactions indicate her influence and authority within the compound's magical and political dynamics.

Session 28 showed how she left. In the chaos of the Athenaeum's vaults she had found a book from its library holding the working Plane Gate, which opens a way to a place on another plane one has been to before; she saw her chance and took it, and made her door at the top of the Astronomer's Spire, where the great telescope let her see the plane she meant to reach.

It brought her to the World Above. Sylvie found her at the edge of a lake there, singing to something in the water, with a parasite fastened over most of her head and its tendrils in her mouth and nose. Called with the Voice of Dread, she turned and said Sylvie's name. As she started toward her up the stone steps, the thing in the lake — a crocodile of impossible size, all mouths and teeth — swept her off them with its tail and took her under. She did not come up, and no one went in after her. Inola is dead. The book of Plane Gate lay under a bush near the lake; Sylvie learned it.
