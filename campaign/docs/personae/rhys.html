Dramatis Persona

Rhys

Rhys appears undead, as noted by Sylvie during their encounter. He has a history as a fellow stone-weaver alongside Sylvie and believes the world was dead until Sylvie's actions brought them back. Rhys is connected to a powerful entity referred to as the Runt God, which he claims is the source of their power in the upper world. He desires to find and wrangle the Runt God to ascend with it, showing commitment to this goal despite potential consequences.

Rhys is a stone weaver who has previously been in conflict with the party but has reached a detente. He is associated with Kaelyn, another stone weaver, and both were involved in a conflict with the party regarding a creature known as the Runt God. Rhys and Kaelyn utilized the power of the Runt God to thrive in the World Above. Interactions with the party reveal a complex relationship, characterized by past antagonism and potential for cooperation. At one point, Sylvie told Rhys to “f*** off and die,” indicating a contentious dynamic between them.

Rhys engages in a tense exchange with Sylvie, asking for help against a towering eldritch creature controlled by Kaelyn. He states, "Help me. Kaelyn had its leash," indicating his vulnerability. During the battle, he projects a sustained magical beam at the creature while two Stone Elementals flank him for protection. After the creature vanishes, Rhys approaches Sylvie, confirming her survival and expressing the need to find the Runt God. He admits to having followed Kaelyn blindly in the past and describes Sylvie's previous response to a confrontation as powerful and transformative. During a long rest, he explains the nature of the Runt God and the necessity of ascending with the other gods, arguing for the importance of their survival and power.

In Session 28, in the World Above, Sylvie brought him back. She charged an old Stoneweaver crystal with the loose Splendor there and reached for him as she reaches for others in sleep; the working went through only when she gave up the power of damnation Ygva had given her. Rhys walked out of the chest of a dead sphinx, covered in honey, and took her hands as if he did not believe she was real. He is solid; not warm, but not a corpse. I knew you would make it up here, he told her. Where had he been? Between places.

He did not know that Sylvie had killed him, and took the news calmly: you appear to have remedied that mistake. We should probably talk about all of that sometime. He believes he can leave the World Above with the company and bring them back to it, and will come with them. For now he travels with them and is reading Sylvie's books, to help her think through the right path forward for them and for the world.
