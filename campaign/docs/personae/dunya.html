Dramatis Persona

Dunya

Dunya is an undead dwarf with a spectral form, reflecting her status as a revenant. She appears among Jamal's friends and neighbors in Grithmaar Deep.

Upon seeing Sylvie, Dunya initially screams with rage but hesitates as she recognizes Jamal. Her presence in the flooded ceremonial hall adds to the tension of the encounter, particularly as Jamal confronts familiar faces in their undead state. Dunya shows wariness towards Sylvie's radiant aura, indicating that Sylvie's light significantly affects the undead.
