Location

Ygva’s Peak

Ygva's Peak is a notable location defined by a mystical fire linked to Ygva, a towering 7.5-foot witch. The area includes a padded bedroll, indicating its use for rest and potential healing. This site is significant for supernatural encounters and cosmic revelations regarding reality and the influence of powerful entities. The surrounding environment conveys an otherworldly atmosphere where life and death intersect, allowing for profound transformations.

Access to Ygva's Peak involves navigating treacherous mountain paths and crossing a rope bridge affected by supernatural forces. Upon arrival, the party encountered Ygva, who teleported into their midst, leading to a violent confrontation. Ygva captured Sylvie and transported her to Ygva's Peak, where she regained consciousness to find Ygva. In this moment, Ygva divulged cosmic truths about their world being an "egg" for departed entities and presented Sylvie with an ultimatum concerning her magic. The encounter intensified when Draz attempted a rescue, resulting in chaos during their conversation and culminating in Ygva extracting Sylvie's heart and taking several Blessed Branch, marking a moment of transformation and loss at Ygva's Peak.

The Mountainside Camp

Below the peak lies Ygva's open-sided mountainside camp, set at a higher elevation with raised platforms and an overhang for shelter from the elements. Its fire arrangements are ordinary, indicating practical use rather than ritual. Various witchcraft supplies are present, including cupboards filled with dried berries, powders, leaves, and branches, though there are no maps or notes. Small Pictogram Stones, some arranged on a flat stone, may hint at a connection to the witch's practices or history.

The camp was the site of an ambush by shadowy figures and a giant tick, leading to a confrontation of spells and physical attacks. Following this threat, the party chose to explore the southwestern path away from the witch and her potential dangers.
