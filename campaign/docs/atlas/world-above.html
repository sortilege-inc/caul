World Above

The destination of Ascension referenced by Inola, who proposed bringing ascended elect there, and described as reachable only by The Elect.

Reached through the rupture the company had always called the moon (Session 27). Standing at its lip you can look down through it and see the whole world below. There is no Umbra there; Splendor hangs loose in the air like light with no sun to cast it. It is a younger world than theirs, at an earlier stage of itself, running on far more power — and everything in it is vast and dangerous. Being there made the company stronger (Session 28).

Inola came here through Plane Gate and died here, taken into a lake. Mollthain passed through it on its way down to the world below.
