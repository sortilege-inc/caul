Relic

Noxious Powders

Noxious Powders are concentrated chemical substances found within a mixed herbalism and alchemy study. When Draz opened a box containing these powders, he released concentrated chemical fumes that caused coughing and stinging eyes. Despite the discomfort, he chose to take the box with him.

Draz encountered the Noxious Powders while exploring a Scholar Statue Chamber equipped with various tools for working with powdered substances. After inhaling the fumes, he slammed the box shut and decided to carry it, resulting in stress damage due to the exposure.
