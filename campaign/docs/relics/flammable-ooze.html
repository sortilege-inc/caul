Relic

Flammable Ooze

Flammable ooze is a volatile substance harvested from the corpse of an ogre. Its primary function is to serve as an explosive agent when ignited.

During an ambush, the ooze was strategically spread throughout the zone. Draz ignited it with a flaming arrow, resulting in an explosion that killed one goblin, caused panic among two others, injured another who fled, and left one survivor attempting to extinguish flames. The explosion signaled the start of combat for the rest of the party. This use of flammable ooze highlighted its effectiveness in combat strategy.
